# B003 — The evidence-backed learner model is the core compounding asset

## Position

Content becomes abundant; trustworthy knowledge about what a person can do, how that estimate was formed, and what experience may help next remains scarce. That evidence can compound across interactions if it remains interpretable and revisable.

## Why we believe this

Adaptive guidance requires domain-specific learner evidence [C003]. Learner models are probabilistic, validity-laden inferences [C007], and consequential judgments need multiple evidence channels [C008]. Pedagogical design, not raw model fluency, changes outcomes [C002].

## Strongest counterarguments

Personalization is frequently overclaimed. An opaque model may create false authority, privacy risk, and brittle paths. Strong default sequences may beat complex adaptation.

## What would change our mind

We would demote the learner model from the product core if pre-registered comparisons showed no meaningful learning or decision advantage over transparent non-adaptive designs.

## Implications

Invest in evidence provenance, uncertainty, calibration, construct maps, temporal decay, and user contestability before sophisticated recommendation algorithms.
