# B001 — Optimize durable capability, not completion

## Position

The learning engine earns its name only when its objective function extends beyond producing a good artifact today. It should help a person become more capable tomorrow and reveal when it is operating as performance support instead.

## Why we believe this

Assisted performance can diverge from unaided learning [C001]. Structured pedagogy can improve outcomes [C002], and retrieval and spacing show why slower or harder-looking experiences may create more durable memory [C004, C005].

## Strongest counterarguments

People often need to perform now, and AI-mediated collaboration is itself an authentic future capability. The answer is not to reject assistance; it is to distinguish modes and measure the capability each mode claims to develop.

## What would change our mind

We would narrow this belief if reliable longitudinal evidence showed that immediate AI-assisted success was sufficient for the consequential decisions a product makes, or if independent measurement created greater harm than value in a particular setting.

## Implications

The product should declare learning, assessment, and performance-support modes; maintain separate outcomes; and privilege delayed independent and transfer evidence when making a learning claim.
