# B002 — Preserve productive effort while removing incidental friction

## Position

Effort is neither automatically virtuous nor automatically waste. The design task is to protect the thinking that changes the learner while removing administrative, accessibility, and interface burdens that do not belong to the target capability.

## Why we believe this

Answer provision can substitute for learning [C001]; retrieval creates learning precisely by requiring generation [C004]; and the useful amount of guidance varies with expertise [C003]. Structured AI can improve learning when it supports rather than bypasses the process [C002].

## Strongest counterarguments

The system cannot observe cognition directly, and its attempt to create “desirable difficulty” can become paternalistic or inaccessible. Learner goals and time pressure matter.

## What would change our mind

We would revise a particular friction judgment when independent outcomes fail to improve, learners cannot access the task equitably, or the effort is unrelated to the declared construct.

## Implications

Use attempt-before-answer, progressive hints, worked-example fading, retrieval, and reflection conditionally. Offer explicit performance support rather than pretending all friction serves learning.
