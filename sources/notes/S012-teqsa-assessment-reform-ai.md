# S012 — Enacting Assessment Reform in a Time of Artificial Intelligence

## Why it matters

The guidance treats assessment reform as a system-design problem under ubiquitous AI, not merely a detection problem. It is directly relevant to trustworthy capability judgments in higher education.

## Argument

The report advocates programme-level assessment design using multiple, inclusive, and contextualized approaches. Trustworthy judgments should draw on accumulated evidence and purposeful opportunities for students to demonstrate capability under different conditions, including appropriate AI use.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

This is expert and sector guidance rather than a causal trial of one assessment architecture. Institutions differ in discipline, regulation, resources, and risk. The recommendations require operational validation and careful treatment of equity and accessibility.
