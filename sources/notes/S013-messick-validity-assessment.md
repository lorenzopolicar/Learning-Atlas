# S013 — Validity of Psychological Assessment

## Why it matters

It redirects attention from whether a score is “valid” in isolation to whether the interpretation and use of evidence are justified, including their consequences.

## Argument

Messick presents validity as an integrated evaluative judgment about the adequacy and appropriateness of interpretations and actions based on assessment evidence. Construct representation, irrelevant variance, value implications, and consequences all matter.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

This is foundational theory, not a recipe for validating an AI learner model. Later standards and debates refine the framework. Specific system claims still require an explicit validation argument and empirical evidence.
