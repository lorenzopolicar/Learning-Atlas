# S010 — The Power of Feedback

## Why it matters

Generative systems can produce unlimited feedback; the educational challenge is selecting feedback that changes the learner’s next action without replacing the thinking.

## Synthesis

The review organizes feedback around the questions “Where am I going?”, “How am I going?”, and “Where to next?”, and distinguishes feedback about task, process, self-regulation, and self. Feedback effects vary substantially and can be positive or negative depending on focus, timing, interpretation, and context.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

The synthesis predates generative AI and covers heterogeneous studies. Its taxonomy is a useful design lens, not a guarantee that every process-level prompt improves learning. Feedback uptake and learner trust must be measured.
