# S001 — Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning outcomes

## Why it matters

This study directly separates performance while AI is available from later performance after access is removed. It is unusually relevant to the risk that an AI learning product optimizes visible completion while eroding independent capability.

## Study

A preregistered field experiment involved nearly 1,000 students in high-school mathematics classes. Students received access to either a general-purpose GPT interface, a tutor with pedagogical guardrails, or no AI during practice. A later exam removed AI access.

## Findings

Both AI conditions improved supported practice performance. Students who had used the unrestricted interface subsequently performed worse without it than the control group. The safeguarded tutor substantially reduced this negative learning effect. The result supports neither “AI harms learning” nor “AI tutors work” in general; it shows that interaction design can change the relationship between assisted performance and independent performance.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

The work covers a particular mathematics curriculum, age group, implementation, and relatively short horizon. Treatment contrast combines several interface and pedagogical differences. It does not establish long-term retention, far transfer, or broad generality across subjects and learners.

## Evidence profile

| Dimension | Rating | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Internal validity | high | Preregistered randomized field design with a no-AI comparison |
| Directness | high | Directly compares AI-assisted practice with independent assessment |
| Consistency | moderate | Direction aligns with the broader learning/performance distinction; exact intervention needs replication |
| Replication | low | The specific treatment contrast is not yet independently replicated here |
| Magnitude | moderate | Practically meaningful differences were reported |
| Duration | low | Follow-up is short |
| Transfer | low | Assessment remains close to the taught mathematics |
| Ecological validity | high | Conducted in authentic classrooms |
