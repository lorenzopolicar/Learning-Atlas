# S011 — Knowledge Tracing

## Why it matters

Knowledge tracing is an early influential account of updating estimates of skill mastery from learner opportunities and responses. It offers both a useful architecture and a warning: a learner model is an inference under assumptions, not a transcript of the mind.

## Model

The work models acquisition of procedural knowledge over successive practice opportunities using latent mastery and probabilities for learning, guessing, and slipping. These estimates can support adaptive problem selection and tutoring decisions.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

Model quality depends on skill decomposition, observation quality, stationarity assumptions, and fit between logged responses and the target construct. Correctness can reflect guessing, assistance, collaboration, or flawed items. Modern models may predict better while remaining difficult to interpret or validate as capability measures.
