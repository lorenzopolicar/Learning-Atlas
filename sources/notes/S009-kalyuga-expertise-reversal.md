# S009 — The Expertise Reversal Effect

## Why it matters

Personalization cannot mean “more guidance for everyone.” Support that reduces extraneous load for a novice may become redundant, inefficient, or harmful as expertise grows.

## Review and argument

The paper synthesizes evidence that the effectiveness of instructional techniques interacts with learner expertise. Detailed guidance, worked information, and explanatory support can help novices but impose redundant processing on more knowledgeable learners.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

Expertise is domain-specific and difficult to infer reliably. The effect does not imply abandoning all support for experts or linearly reducing help. Task novelty, element interactivity, goals, and assessment quality matter.
