# S003 — Building AI Companions that Prioritise Learning over Performance

## Why it matters

This preprint provides a design vocabulary for learning-first AI companions across pedagogical, adaptive, and responsible foundations. It closely matches the atlas distinction between output assistance and capability development.

## Argument

The framework argues that educational companions should diagnose and adapt, preserve learner agency, use evidence-based pedagogy, and optimize learning rather than task completion. It connects design decisions across interaction, learner modelling, and responsibility rather than presenting a single tutoring technique.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

This is a recent preprint and framework, not itself causal evidence that the full framework improves learning. Its components need empirical testing, operational definitions, and comparison with alternative architectures.
