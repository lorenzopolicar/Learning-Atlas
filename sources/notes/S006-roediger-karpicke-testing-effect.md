# S006 — Test-Enhanced Learning

## Why it matters

The experiments demonstrate the classic divergence between conditions that look best immediately and those that produce better delayed retention.

## Study and findings

Students studied prose passages and then either restudied or practised retrieval. Restudy could benefit very short-delay performance, while prior retrieval produced stronger retention after longer delays. Retrieval is therefore both an assessment event and a learning event under appropriate conditions.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

Laboratory prose learning is narrower than authentic capability. Retrieval quality, corrective feedback, knowledge type, test format, and delay matter. Retrieval practice is not a warrant for constant high-stakes testing.
