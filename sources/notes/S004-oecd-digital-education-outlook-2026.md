# S004 — OECD Digital Education Outlook 2026

## Why it matters

The report synthesizes emerging evidence and policy questions about effective generative-AI use in education. It foregrounds a recurring pattern: general-purpose AI may improve the quality of immediate work without producing equivalent learning.

## Synthesis

The OECD distinguishes generic task assistance from tools and practices designed with pedagogical intent. It discusses roles for teachers, institutions, evaluation, and governance, and emphasizes that educational value depends on use and design rather than access alone.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

This is a broad institutional synthesis, not one homogeneous systematic review or causal study. The evidence base is fast moving, and examples vary in maturity, populations, outcomes, and methods. Follow primary citations before using a specific reported effect.
