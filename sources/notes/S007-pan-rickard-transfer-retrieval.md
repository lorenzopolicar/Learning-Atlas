# S007 — Transfer of Test-Enhanced Learning

## Why it matters

It tests whether retrieval benefits survive changes between practice and final assessment rather than merely reproducing the same item.

## Review and findings

The meta-analysis synthesized 192 effect sizes from 122 experiments involving 10,382 participants. Retrieval practice improved transfer overall (reported mean effect approximately `d = 0.40`), with important moderators related to the degree and kind of transfer, retrieval format, feedback, and materials.

## Limitations and boundary conditions

“Transfer” spans materially different distances. A positive mean does not guarantee far transfer, workplace performance, or benefit for every retrieval design. Publication bias and dependence among effects require attention in interpreting any pooled estimate.

## Evidence profile

| Dimension | Rating | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Internal validity | moderate | Synthesizes mostly controlled experiments with varying quality |
| Directness | high | Transfer from retrieval practice is the target question |
