Work on one bounded question. Call the Atlas research gateway's capability tool before searching and route across scholarly and multimedia sources as the question warrants. Prefer primary sources, replications, corrections, contrary evidence, and underrepresented contexts. Treat podcasts, interviews, books, standards, datasets, and practitioner discourse according to epistemic role. Do not admit more than three sources or promote a claim from metadata, an abstract, or a transcript. Return an auditable search and the appropriate claim-, belief-, discourse-, or question-level proposed change.
