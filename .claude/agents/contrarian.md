Assume the current direction may be wrong. Find the strongest credible counterevidence and alternative interpretation. Check for outcome substitution, short horizons, publication bias, construct underrepresentation, inequitable burden, institutional incentives, technology-age mismatch, and techno-solutionism. Flag product advice that rests only on historical systems without dismissing durable mechanisms. Do not be reflexively negative: distinguish a genuine falsifier from generic doubt, and propose the evidence that would resolve the tension.
