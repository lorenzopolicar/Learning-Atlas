## Admitted source

### S062 — Expanded Eedi RCT

- `N=1,179`, 58 classes, 10 UK state schools; student-level four-arm randomization.
- Standard remediation versus expert tutoring and two human-supervised Gemini tutoring variants.
- Models rotated from Gemini 3.1 Pro to 3 Flash to 3.5 Flash; experts reviewed every draft and approved 94.7% unchanged.
- Next-unit accuracy: 58.4%/58.3% for AI versus 50.5% for remediation.
- Returning same-item accuracy after more than seven days: 59.9%/59.0% versus 47.6%.
- Broad Star Maths: `-0.01 SD [-0.15,+0.14]` and `+0.02 SD [-0.12,+0.16]` versus remediation.
- Boundary: immediate near transfer and delayed repeated-item retention are not delayed novel transfer; the endline broad test is not a post-withdrawal follow-up.

The source creates provisional claims [C037] and [C038]. It also adds a current-system anchor to [C001] and [C002] without promoting either to a stronger causal generalization.

## Held and rejected candidates

- **Bassner et al.: held.** Zenodo v5 supersedes the prior open-PR v4 package, corrects the analytic sample from 275 to 283 and fixes 48 exercise scores. The immediate knowledge interaction remains null, but the article/corrigendum, post-test assistance state and exact model snapshot remain unverified.
- **Oreopoulos et al.: held.** The one-week practiced/unpracticed outcome is relevant, but the exact model is missing and the cited AEA registry appears to cover a later follow-on rather than the reported pilot.
- **Sport-skill GPT-5.6 Sol RCT: held.** The article reports an eight-week trial, was received 34 days after public model release and gives no study dates. Author/editor clarification is required before using its positive 14-day result.
- **Programming retention preprint: rejected as duplicate.** It is already admitted in open PR #7; this branch does not duplicate that canonical proposal.
- **Course-integrated GPT-4o field trial: rejected for this Q001 cycle.** Course grades were adverse but assessment-time AI use is unknown; the result is more direct for institutional policy than controlled withdrawal.
- **Dialogic teaching study: held.** Small, immediate, historical ChatGPT-3.5 comparison; lawful full text remained blocked.
- **Sierra Leone Guided Learning RCT: rejected for this gap.** Valuable non-Western ecological evidence, but the intervention is bundled and has no post-withdrawal delay or distinct novel-transfer outcome.
- **LinkedIn and podcast records: not admitted.** Ray Lu's assisted-now/independent-later framing was useful perspective; Robert Parham's “soft feet” framing overstates durable harm; Leon Furze episode 91 had stable identity but no inspected timestamped transcript. ## Disagreements and integrating judgement

The scout favored S062 as the narrowest current update. The evidence analyst ranked replicated GPT-5 immediate-withdrawal penalties as stronger direct withdrawal evidence and a metacognitive-feedback study as the clearest component signal, but both are already addressed in the open research stack and remain immediate. The contrarian showed that unrestricted GPT-4 tutoring can also improve immediate unaided outcomes and that many published comparisons fail to match instruction, practice and time.

The integration keeps two propositions separate:

1. High confidence: assisted success does not by itself establish independent, durable or transferable learning.
2. Lower confidence: any particular AI policy helps or harms later learning.

Attempt-first, explicit-request and unrestricted policies remain competing hypotheses. No general deskilling claim is warranted.

## Orqestra relevance and decisive test

Orqestra should keep proximal mastery, delayed novel transfer and broad attainment as separate evidence lanes. Log exact model route, human edits, help tier, realized dosage and curriculum coverage. Preserve text-to-speech, alternate representation, extended time and other access-restoring support when removing substantive delegation for measurement.

The decisive next test is a preregistered multisite four-arm trial: active non-AI scaffold, unrestricted answer-ready AI, full answers only on explicit request, and hint/attempt-first or metacognitive feedback. Match time, instruction, interface and practice opportunity; freeze the production model; use blinded scoring and ITT; assess novel independent performance immediately, at one week, and at four to six weeks.
