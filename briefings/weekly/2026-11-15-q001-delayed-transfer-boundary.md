## Admitted sources

### S063 — Delayed engineering transfer

- 656 students, 28 classes, nine instructors and six Uzbek universities.
- Sixteen-week attempt-first, compare-and-verify GPT-4o workflow versus the same workflow using curated alternatives.
- Supervised unfamiliar tool-free immediate and delayed forms.
- Adjusted differences: `+2.78/32 [2.02,3.55]` immediate and `+2.34/32 [1.54,3.14]` delayed.
- Boundary: deterministic post-baseline class allocation, bundled adaptivity/dialogue, no public prospective registration, author-developed outcome and historical GPT-4o.

### S064 — Current-system assisted/no-AI writing contrast

- 168 completers in six intact Chinese university English-writing classes.
- GPT-5.3 open collaboration versus bounded support plus compulsory reflection versus no AI.
- Open collaboration had the highest supported product mean; bounded support had the highest observed Week 8 supervised no-AI near-transfer profile.
- Exact wild-cluster p values for the decisive contrasts ranged from `.050` to `.063`.
- Boundary: class confounding, only six clusters, bundled treatment, immediate same-genre near transfer and no delayed outcome.

## Claim-level change and Orqestra relevance

C001 keeps its wording and high confidence. [S064] illustrates the measurement boundary without supporting a directional harm claim. C002 stays provisional and now states more explicitly that neither study isolates attempt timing, reflection, verification, adaptivity or model effects.

For Orqestra, the next test should compare a matched transparent non-AI scaffold, answer-ready AI, bounded AI with separately varied reflection/verification, and learner-controlled access. Freeze and log the production model, match time and practice, preserve access-restoring support, score assisted and immediate independent performance separately, and repeat novel independent tasks at one and four to six weeks.

## Uncertainty and decisive falsifier

The strongest falsifier is a preregistered multisite trial using a frozen current model, matched active non-AI control, randomized policy components, verified assistance withdrawal, blinded scoring, complete-follow-up sensitivity analysis, and separately scored assisted, immediate independent and delayed novel independent outcomes.

Until that exists, the Atlas should not claim that open AI generally harms learning, that bounded support generally helps, or that attempt-first/verification is the active ingredient.
