## Answer

Keep **AI-mediated task stewardship** as narrow experimental language, but do not mistake it for an account of educational agency or purpose. Treat **capability sovereignty** as a normative, relational and institutionally distributed lens:

- education acts across qualification, socialization and subjectification; measurable performance cannot settle all three, and subjectification must not become a product score [S048];
- tools, observed outputs and choices are resources or functionings, not proof of substantive capability without real alternatives and conversion conditions [S049];
- AI availability, permanence and institutional role are governed choices, not facts of nature. Infrastructure, labour, surveillance and material inequality shape who bears dependency and burden [S050];
- competent individual use cannot compensate for a compulsory, opaque or dependency-producing institution;
- record correction is procedural contestability, not democratic voice. Co-design must expose a material decision participants can change;
- a successful E001 pilot could validate a bounded evidence procedure, but not educational purpose, subjectification, substantive freedom or institutional adoption.

## Orqestra relevance and falsification plan

Before institutional adoption, require a bounded purpose-and-governance rehearsal:

1. learners and educators can change at least one material design or use decision;
2. a credible non-AI or differently mediated route exists without punitive loss;
3. provider/model and evidence portability make exit operational rather than rhetorical;
4. verification, explanation and recovery obligations are allocated across learner, educator, institution and vendor;
5. teacher workload, privacy/data burden and unequal conversion conditions are measured;
6. a richer evidence ledger is compared with a purpose-minimal baseline for incremental decision value, subgroup calibration/invariance and later accessible-independent or transfer outcomes.

Narrow or retire the product framing if task evidence predicts bounded correctness but not meaningful choice or influence; participants cannot refuse, exit or alter decisions; authentic learner-authored or peer work exposes capabilities the bounded probes miss; the richer ledger adds no decision value; or surveillance and repair burden falls inequitably on learners and educators.

## Uncertainty and limitations

All three admitted sources are argument or perspective, not AI-effect evidence. The portfolio is Western and English-language. Biesta and the capability approach are model-independent and durable but do not decide Orqestra's purpose or governance procedure. Watters is technologically current and direct but polemical; her claims about burden, inequality and infrastructure require comparative institutional evidence. Public deliberation can reproduce power, learner choice can reflect adapted preferences, and child education creates a real tension between present refusal and future capability.

The next falsification step is learner/educator co-design with real decision authority, followed by a purpose-minimal comparative pilot. Seek non-Western and non-English accounts, disabled and otherwise underserved communities, teacher/labour evidence, public digital-infrastructure governance and implementation cases where refusal or portability changed an outcome.
