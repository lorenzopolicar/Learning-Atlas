# Foundation briefing — 2026-08-31

## Signal

The strongest initial direction is a design distinction, not a model ranking: AI can make a learner’s current performance better while leaving independent capability unchanged or worse. Structured tutoring and teacher-supported designs show that this is not inevitable. The pedagogical harness is therefore a causal part of the product.

## What entered the atlas

- 14 source notes spanning recent GenAI field studies, learning-science mechanisms, learner modelling, tutoring, and assessment validity;
- 8 bounded claims;
- 5 adopted founding beliefs;
- 7 active design principles;
- an initial scoping-review protocol and extraction set for the learning/performance gap.

## Direction of travel

The proposed learning engine should optimize durable, transferable capability; represent independent and assisted capability separately; preserve productive cognitive effort; and build a provenance-rich, contestable learner evidence model.

## Caution

The recent intervention evidence is promising but thin, context-specific, and short-term. The atlas must now seek null results, replications, longer delays, transfer, equity effects, and component-level explanations rather than collecting only affirmative AI-tutoring studies.

## Next move

Complete a reproducible post-2025 search for controlled GenAI studies with unassisted outcomes, then expand the learner-model programme from foundational knowledge tracing into contemporary validity, uncertainty, and ethics research.
