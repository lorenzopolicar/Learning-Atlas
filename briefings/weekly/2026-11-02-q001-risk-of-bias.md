## Question advanced

Under which learner, task, interaction and assessment conditions does generative-AI assistance improve immediate performance without durable independent learning, and which interventions reduce that gap?

This run tested whether a delayed adverse study, a workplace assisted/unaided dissociation and formal result-level bias appraisal should change the seed synthesis.

## Admissions, holds and rejections

### Admitted

1. [S060] Barcauí, *ChatGPT as a Cognitive Crutch*. The published open article reports 120 randomized learners and a 45-day proctored score of 5.75 versus 6.85 among 85 completers (`d=.68`). It was admitted because the delayed independent outcome changes the answer, not because the direction is adverse.
2. [S061] Wiles et al., *Generative AI and the Temporary Upskilling of Knowledge Workers*. The preregistered workplace trial reports large assisted gains but no joint immediate unaided knowledge difference (`F(5,198)=0.83`, `p=.531`). It extends the measurement boundary into workplace capability.

### Held or rejected

- Bassner et al., DOI `10.1016/j.caeai.2025.100537`: hold. The lawful Zenodo v4 package exposed tasks, data, exclusions and analysis code, including 452 raw to 275 analytic participants and null differential knowledge results. The article, exact model identity and preregistration remained unavailable; the 39.2% exclusions and absence of a delayed outcome limit its contribution.
- Kreijkes et al., DOI `10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105514`: future hold. The preregistered school study has three-day independent outcomes, but its GPT-3.5 intervention is historical and the full-text route was unresolved.
- Liu et al., DOI `10.26300/y3f8-vh05`: hold for institutional transfer. Section/instructor allocation, low uptake and widespread other-AI use make it a poor direct test of this question.
- Alam et al.: reject from canonical admission in this pass. The exploratory `N=13` sample is too fragile to change the answer.
- Sources S057–S059 on open draft PR #7 were not duplicated. They are part of the publication-state context, not this independent branch.
- The LinkedIn post and podcast were not admitted as empirical evidence.

No third source was admitted merely to fill the cap.

## Independent agent comparison

The research scout prioritized current controlled and delayed candidates. The evidence analyst independently extracted estimands, outcome timing and missingness. The contrarian challenged any broad causal or mechanism claim. All lanes agreed that Barcauí and Wiles change the boundary but do not justify a general harm verdict. Discovery rewarded the rarity and delay of Barcauí; falsification treated its 35 missing outcomes and unmonitored comparator compliance as decisive confidence limits. The integrating judgement admits the source but creates only low-confidence [C036].

Two appraisal disagreements changed the integrating judgement. The evidence analyst rated [S001] `some concerns`, rather than the initial `low`, because stochastic arm assignment and concealment are not explicit. The analyst rated [S023] `moderate`, rather than the initial `serious`, because its within-person rotation reduces confounding and all participants completed. Both challenges were accepted in the final pilot labels, while the disagreement history remains explicit for duplicate adjudication.

The lanes agreed that [C001] is a measurement claim and survives, whereas [C002] is more vulnerable because access, model, interface, time, teacher role and incentives are bundled. The contrarian recommended against strengthening [P001]; that recommendation was accepted.

## Claim-level change

- [C001] remains high-confidence as a measurement boundary: assisted performance is not evidence of independent learning. [S061] adds a workplace dissociation; [S060] adds a delayed adverse result.
- [C002] remains provisional. Most causal contrasts retain some, high or serious concerns; S023 is moderate risk under ROBINS-I V2. Every label still awaits independent duplicate adjudication.
- New [C036] is low confidence and deliberately narrow: one Brazilian randomized trial found lower 45-day retention under unrestricted GPT-4-labelled study access. It does not establish cognitive offloading as the mechanism or generalize to current systems.

The result-level pilot uses RoB 2 variants for randomized designs and draft ROBINS-I V2 for non-randomized designs. It is not duplicate-adjudicated and does not support pooling.

## Orqestra relevance

Orqestra should treat unrestricted answer-ready assistance as a testable risk, not a neutral default or proven universal harm. The next useful product experiment records exposure and compares answer-ready, effort-preserving and learner-controlled policies on delayed novel independent performance. Reduced study time is an outcome of the policy, not by itself proof of cognitive offloading. Access-restoring support must remain distinct from answer substitution.

## Uncertainty and missing evidence

There is still no credible current-system multisite study with delayed novel transfer, monitored exposure and accessible-independent assessment. Disability/accessibility representation, non-Western replication beyond one Brazilian cohort, clean policy-component isolation, trial-registry coverage and publication-bias mapping remain weak. The 45-day result could move materially after missing-outcome data, control contamination or independent replication is resolved.
