# R001 — Learning versus performance in AI-assisted learning

## Review question

Under which learner, task, interaction, and assessment conditions does generative-AI assistance improve immediate task performance without durable independent learning, and which interventions reduce that gap?

## Review type and stage

Living scoping review with systematic search and transparent reporting. The initial purpose is to map constructs, designs, outcomes, and gaps; a later narrow causal question may warrant a systematic review and meta-analysis. PRISMA-ScR/PRISMA 2020 guide reporting, while the JBI scoping-review methodology guides conduct.

## Conceptual inclusion criteria

- **Population:** learners of any age in formal, workplace, or structured informal learning.
- **Concept:** generative-AI or LLM assistance used during learning, practice, feedback, tutoring, or assessment preparation.
- **Context:** any subject or domain; retain setting and stakes as moderators.
- **Evidence:** empirical comparative studies must report a learning-relevant outcome. Prioritize designs that separate supported activity from unassisted assessment and include delay or transfer.
- **Time:** retain 2022 onward for the historical GenAI map, but search a rolling 18-month technology-current stratum first for product-direct conclusions. Record data-collection period and system version separately from publication year. Earlier tutoring and learning-science evidence enters through background reviews and mechanism chains.
- **Language:** English for the initial pass; record this limitation.

## Exclusion criteria

- output-quality or satisfaction evaluations with no learner outcome;
- opinion pieces presented as intervention evidence;
- AI-detection studies with no learning outcome;
- generic personalization not involving a generative system, except clearly labelled background evidence;
- inaccessible records for which methods and outcomes cannot be verified.

## Outcomes

Primary outcome categories:

1. immediate independent performance;
2. delayed retention;
3. near transfer;
4. far transfer or authentic capability.

Secondary outcomes include assisted task performance, time, engagement, motivation, calibration, agency, help-seeking, dependence, equity, and adverse effects.

## Planned process

1. Search and deduplicate records; preserve exact queries and dates.
2. Screen title/abstract, then full text, recording exclusion reasons.
3. Extract context, intervention components, comparison, assistance state, outcomes, timing, effect information, risks of bias, and funding/conflicts.
4. Map outcome definitions and intervention mechanisms before grouping results.
5. Synthesize direction and boundary conditions; do not pool incomparable outcomes.
6. Grade the evidence profile by claim, not by paper count.
7. Update claims and explicitly record contradictions.
8. Report technology freshness by claim and identify conclusions supported only by historical systems.

## Bias and quality appraisal

Use RoB 2 for randomized trials when a mature synthesis requires formal appraisal. For other designs, use an appropriate design-specific tool. The atlas evidence profile complements but does not replace risk-of-bias assessment.

## Current status

The orientation set now includes direct assisted/independent contrasts [S001, S015, S023, S024], structured tutoring and programme studies [S002, S005], the authentic point-of-care trial [S025], and the pre-GenAI tutoring review [S014]. The 2026-09-01 targeted update added one null/inconclusive one-week programming contrast, one fragile positive access-timing contrast, and one authentic clinical null. This remains a seed synthesis, not a database-complete review. The next run must complete reproducible database and citation-chain searches, resolve the full text of the 275-participant Bassner et al. null RCT, and formally appraise risk of bias.

## Governance

Protocol changes are versioned before applying them to screening. Automated runs may propose inclusions and extractions but cannot mark the review complete or promote a contested claim without human review.
