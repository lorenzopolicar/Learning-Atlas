# Artifact lifecycle

## Intake

A question enters `research/queue.md`. A scout searches narrowly, logs the search, and proposes sources. Discovery metadata is not yet evidence.

## Admission

A source receives an `S` identifier only after a human or agent has inspected enough of the actual work to create a useful note. Automated runs add no more than three sources.

## Extraction

The analyst records study context, results, limitations, and an evidence profile. Claims remain atomic and link to the source ID.

## Synthesis

Claims may update a belief or principle when evidence is sufficiently direct and the reasoning is explicit. Contradictions are preserved. Scheduled synthesis proposes changes in a pull request; a human publishes them.

## Application

Product teams query the atlas and cite the relevant IDs in a decision. Product observations flow back as experiments or questions, not retroactive proof.

## Maintenance

Artifacts older than the configured review interval are flagged. Retirement preserves the file and history. Generated indexes and graph exports are reproducible views, never the authoritative record.
