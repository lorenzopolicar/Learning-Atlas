# Evidence appraisal rubric

For each empirical source, answer these in the source note. `Unclear` is preferable to invented certainty.

## Study identity

- What exact question was studied?
- What were the population, setting, sample, and recruitment process?
- What intervention and comparator were used?
- Which outcomes were measured, when, and with what instruments?
- Was the work preregistered, peer reviewed, replicated, or corrected?

## Bias and inference

- Was allocation randomized or otherwise credibly controlled?
- Were attrition, exclusions, missing data, and multiplicity handled transparently?
- Could treatment contamination, novelty, instructor effects, or differential time-on-task explain the result?
- Do the reported statistics justify the authors’ causal language?
- Are outcomes independent and delayed, or produced with the intervention still available?

## Relevance to the atlas

- Which atlas claim can this source support or challenge?
- What is the narrowest defensible wording of that claim?
- Where should the result not be generalized?
- What product implication is plausible, and what additional evidence is needed before acting?

## Profile

Record `high`, `moderate`, `low`, `unclear`, or `not-applicable` for internal validity, directness, consistency, replication, magnitude, duration, transfer, and ecological validity. Add one sentence of reasoning for each non-obvious rating.
