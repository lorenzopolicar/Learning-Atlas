# Q001 — When does AI-assisted performance become durable learning?

## Why this matters

It is the highest-leverage question for a system that can either amplify capability or conceal its absence. It anchors review [R001].

## Scope and definitions

Compare supported performance with immediate independent, delayed retention, near transfer, and far transfer. Code learner expertise, subject, interaction policy, answer availability, feedback, teacher role, duration, stakes, and assessment alignment.

## What would answer it

A cumulative set of credible comparative studies, preferably with delayed unassisted outcomes, plus mechanism experiments that isolate pedagogical components.

## Next search

Duplicate the provisional result-level risk-of-bias appraisal, resolve the held Bassner methods, search registries for missing nulls, and prioritize current-system studies with delayed novel independent outcomes. Treat the 45-day adverse result [C036] as a replication target, not a general verdict.
