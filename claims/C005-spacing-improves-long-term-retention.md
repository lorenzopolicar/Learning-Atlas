# C005 — Spacing improves long-term retention

## Evidence and reasoning

A synthesis of 317 experiments finds a broad benefit from distributed over massed practice and shows that the useful inter-study gap changes with the target retention interval [S008].

## Boundary conditions

Recall findings do not automatically establish complex-skill transfer. Real systems must balance theoretical schedules with availability, motivation, urgency, and the costs of returning.

## Product relevance

Maintain an evidence-aware revisit queue. Choose spacing against the time horizon at which capability is meant to remain available, and measure whether learners actually return.
