# C006 — Feedback is not uniformly beneficial

## Evidence and reasoning

Feedback can be powerful, but its effects range from positive to negative. Task, process, and self-regulation information serve different functions, while praise or person-level evaluation can divert attention from the work [S010].

## Boundary conditions and uncertainty

Generative feedback has new properties: abundance, conversational revision, plausible errors, and uncertain learner trust. These require direct contemporary studies rather than simple transfer of older averages.

## Product relevance

Treat feedback as a policy decision. Diagnose before responding, link feedback to goals and next action, create an opportunity to use it, and observe whether the learner revises productively.
