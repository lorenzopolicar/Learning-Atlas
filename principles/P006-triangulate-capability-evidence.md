# P006 — Triangulate capability evidence

## Principle

Use an evidence portfolio proportional to the consequence of the decision. Vary task, time, context, and assistance where these variations matter to the construct; do not convert “more data” into “more truth.”

## Rationale

Learner-state estimates inherit assumptions and error [C007]. AI-era assessment guidance and validity theory support contextualized, programme-level evidence rather than confidence in a single produced artifact [C008].

## Product patterns

- construct maps and evidence requirements by decision;
- independent and supported demonstrations;
- conversation, artifact, process, and performance evidence with explicit roles;
- human review for consequential decisions;
- disagreement and missing-evidence states;
- minimal data collection consistent with the decision.

## Falsifiers and measures

Assess decision accuracy, fairness, consistency, construct coverage, burden, privacy impact, and the marginal value of each evidence channel.
