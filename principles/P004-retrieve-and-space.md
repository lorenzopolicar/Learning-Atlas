# P004 — Retrieve and space important capabilities

## Principle

Create repeated, low-stakes opportunities for the learner to generate knowledge or action from memory, separated over time and varied enough to test the intended transfer.

## Rationale

Retrieval improves delayed retention and some transfer [C004], while distributed practice generally outperforms massing and should be tuned to the retention horizon [C005].

## Product patterns

- a revisit queue keyed to desired retention duration;
- prediction before explanation;
- retrieval with corrective feedback;
- mixed old and new practice;
- short independent probes after assisted work;
- graceful re-entry after missed sessions rather than punitive streaks.

## Falsifiers and measures

Measure delayed independent recall/application, near and far transfer, return rate, burden, and whether spacing rules help beyond a simple periodic default.
