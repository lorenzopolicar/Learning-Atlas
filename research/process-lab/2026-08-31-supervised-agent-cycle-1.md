# Supervised multi-agent proving cycle 1 — 2026-08-31

## Purpose

Exercise the full research-to-product path on a real priority question, compare independent agent judgment, admit no more than three sources, and document what should change before trusting scheduled autonomy.

Question:

> What independent capability must a learner retain when AI can perform the task, and how can independent, AI-assisted and supervisory capability be measured without reflexively banning assistance?

This was a human-directed, supervised shadow cycle. It used the same epistemic and source-cap controls intended for scheduled work, but it is **not** one of the first three actual scheduler executions. Actual scheduled-run observation remains 0/3.

## Agent topology

One integrating agent kept all canonical writes. Three read-only specialists worked independently:

| Lane | Brief | Distinct contribution |
|---|---|---|
| capability measurement | map constructs, tasks, metrics and primary sources | narrowed the operational target to domain-grounded evaluative control; separated reliance directions and confidence quality |
| learner and equity | find learner testimony and test accessibility/fairness assumptions | showed that AI can restore access and that no-tool performance can introduce construct-irrelevant barriers |
| contrarian construct audit | attack novelty, measurability, product misuse and surveillance risk | rejected a sovereignty score and separated a normative relational lens from task-level observations |

After the independent reports, the learner/equity agent acted as a read-only synthesis editor. It compared the reports and recommended a three-source portfolio spanning behaviour, mechanism and lived boundary conditions. The integrating agent made the final admission and artifact decisions.

## Disagreement and resolution

The measurement lane initially preferred Schemmer, Gilbert and Li–Steyvers—a coherent measurement/offloading portfolio. The equity lane argued that this would leave the central accessibility assumption untested and proposed Pierrès et al. The contrarian lane argued that the entire umbrella construct could become a category error and surveillance label.

Resolution:

- admit Schemmer [S020] for two-sided observable reliance;
- admit Li–Steyvers [S021] for the confidence-discrimination mechanism and formal upper bound;
- admit Pierrès et al. [S022] for learner voice and construct-validity boundaries;
- defer Gilbert rather than reject it;
- keep capability sovereignty only as a normative relational lens;
- operationalize a provisional task-level portfolio, not a universal person score [D002, E001].

The disagreement materially improved the result. A simple majority or source-quality rank would likely have produced three technically tidy sources and missed the invalidity of removing access support.

## Orqestra audit result

The existing `CourseEnrollment` and `AssessmentResult` summaries lack task/objective versions, assistance function, accessible baseline, initial judgment, AI provenance, verification, escalation, expiry and permitted-use fields. Bloom and work-profile summaries are too coarse and their current code already documents noise and ceiling effects. The design therefore uses a future append-only evidence contract rather than changing current scores. See the [design brief](../../bridges/orqestra-ai-mediated-stewardship.md).

## What worked

- Read-only specialization prevented write conflicts and preserved independent framing.
- Explicit lane briefs produced genuine disagreement rather than redundant summaries.
- The three-source cap forced portfolio reasoning: each admission needed a different epistemic job.
- Rights receipts, hashes and gitignored full texts kept provenance deterministic while judgment stayed agent-led.
- A contrarian lane prevented a compelling label from becoming an unjustified database field.
- A learner/equity lane changed both construct wording and experiment guardrails rather than becoming a token “limitations” paragraph.

## Friction and limitations

- The specialists still duplicated broad discovery. Future briefs should provide the existing candidate registry and reserve clearly non-overlapping search spaces.
- Project agent definitions were represented through the collaboration runtime rather than invoked by filename. The role instructions still worked, but the first actual Codex/Claude scheduled runs must verify project-agent discovery.
- The formal and behavioural sources concern repeated classification with known truth. The operational design remains weak for open-ended, ethical and long-horizon work.
- One learner-voice study cannot establish population coverage. The current corpus remains English-language and Global-North heavy.
- No actual scheduled automation executed during this supervised run. Worktree creation, draft-PR behaviour, notification quality and scheduler retry handling are still unobserved.
- The three-source cap can exclude a useful synthesis. A future run should admit the Raees review if its map changes search coverage or boundaries rather than merely repeating the primary sources.

## Process improvements for cycle 2

1. Give each specialist the staged candidate registry and explicit `must-not-duplicate` lanes.
2. Require each agent to return one strongest disconfirming source or failure mode, not only recommendations.
3. Add a synthesis matrix with construct, population, task, outcome, assistance function, duration, epistemic role and product implication.
4. Sample at least one non-Western or non-English learner-voice source; record lawful-access failure as a first-class outcome.
5. Randomize which agent acts as synthesis editor to detect role-authority effects.
6. Record elapsed time, candidate overlap, sources inspected/admitted, disagreements preserved and artifact changes for comparison across the first three actual schedules.

## Skill decision

Do not create a new skill after one use. A future `atlas-construct-audit` skill is a candidate if at least one more domain repeats this stable sequence: decompose an attractive construct, assign measurement/equity/contrarian lanes, require incremental validity, and prohibit premature scoring. The existing `consult-learning-atlas` skill is updated now so human-AI capability work retrieves decision and experiment artifacts and applies the accessible-baseline/no-composite guardrails.
